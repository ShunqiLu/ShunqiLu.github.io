## Capo Sfogliato

Copyright (c) 2026 Michele Casanova.

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. See
[assets/fonts/Capo-Sfogliato-OFL.txt](assets/fonts/Capo-Sfogliato-OFL.txt). The
font is taken from the designer's
[Codeberg repository](https://codeberg.org/m-casanova/CapoSfogliato). It is a set
of square foliate initials derived from Petzendorfer's *Schriften-Atlas* (1889),
and this site uses it for one glyph per page: the ornamented capital that opens an
abstract. As permitted by the license, the copy served here is a subset: the
capitals `U+0041-005A` converted to WOFF2, with the lowercase variants, the
ornaments, and the layout features removed. The font is not renamed, as no glyph
outline has been altered.
