Shunqi Lu · Zenodo, v1

Balancing the coordinates of a shift along a fixed \(\ell_1\) shell never decreases lattice overlap. The paper supplies the exact integer increment behind that sign, the sharp rate for Lee balls, and the minimizing orbits on each shell.
